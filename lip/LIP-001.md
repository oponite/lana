# LIP-001: `probability(p)` constructor

- **Status:** Final
- **Author:** —
- **Date:** 2026-09-03

## Motivation

A dedicated `probability(p)` constructor
makes the pure probability of `state(p: p, d: 0.0)` explicit and reduces the surface for a mistaken disposition
value.

## Specification

- `probability(p)` is shorthand for `state(p: p, d: 0.0)`.
- The parser recognizes `probability(...)` as a state constructor; the emitter
  lowers it to `STATE_BUILD p 0.0 0.0` (reusing `emit_state_statement`). No new
  opcode is introduced.
- `state(p:, d:)` and `state(p:, d_re:, d_im:)` remain available and unchanged.
- `CONFIDENT` and `SPECULATIVE` are optional documented presets only; they MUST
  NOT replace or obscure the underlying disposition value.

## Rationale

A pure probability is a degenerate state with zero disposition. Exposing it as a
named constructor documents that degeneracy rather than leaving it implicit in a
`d: 0.0` argument.

## Compatibility

No bytecode or VM change. `probability(p)` lowers to the same `STATE_BUILD` as
`state(p: p, d: 0.0)`, so existing chunks and the LABC v1/v2 contract are
unaffected.

## Test coverage

- Source-language regression: `probability(0.5)` compiles and behaves as
  `state(p: 0.5, d: 0.0)`.
- Bytecode identity: the emitted chunk for `probability(p)` is byte-identical to
  that for `state(p: p, d: 0.0)`.
