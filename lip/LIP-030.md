# LIP-030: Balanced Core information surface

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Author:** Lana maintainers
- **Date:** 2026-09-15

## Motivation

Lana already distinguishes definite values, possibilities, distributions,
joints, and execution paths, but its source surface exposes them unevenly. The
language needs a small explicit algebra rather than implicit probabilities,
silent sampling, or a new general inference engine.

## Specification

- `Information<T>` has five forms: Definite, Possibility, finite Distribution,
  Joint, and Paths. `information`, `possibility`, `distribution`, and typed
  `joint` forms are source origins. Paths arise only from unresolved execution.
- `distribution([[value, weight], ...])` requires nonempty distinct finite
  support, positive finite weights, and a total of one within the established
  tolerance. Materially invalid values fail.
- `condition(info, evidence)` is pure. `observe(info, evidence)` is effectful.
  Joint evidence is a map from names to exact values; the former three-argument
  joint form is a deprecated alias.
- `project` applies only to exact-supported Joint information. `sample` applies
  to Distribution, Joint, and Paths, returns `Sample<T>`, and is read-only.
  Possibility sampling is rejected because it has no probability weights.
- `resolve` succeeds only for definite or singleton information. No operation
  silently resolves, samples, assumes independence, or approximates an exact
  operation. Paths reject condition, observe, and project in 3.0.
- `inspect_information` exposes form, exactness, finite support where known,
  and derivation/evidence metadata without enumerating opaque values.
- New programs using these forms emit LABC v5. The v1-v4 loader and behavior
  remain unchanged.
- `STATE` and `STATE_DIST` remain specialized State-domain values. Their
  mathematical embeddings into Core information do not create implicit source
  conversions or alter State operations.

## Rationale

The matrix makes every form useful while keeping its meaning intact. In
particular, a possibility is a set, not a hidden uniform distribution, and a
path set is execution structure, not a joint law.

## Design test

1. Yes. Constructor and operation names describe their behavior.
2. Yes. Finite sets, weighted distributions, and projections are familiar.
3. No. The existing Information names are retained.
4. Yes. `information(value)` remains the ordinary exact case.
5. Yes. Joint and path behavior is opt-in and inspectable.
6. Yes. `inspect_information` reports form and boundary.
7. Yes. `resolve` and `sample_value` are explicit eliminations.
8. Yes. Sampling and observation are named effects.
9. Yes. Unsupported operations fail instead of guessing.
10. Yes. It fills existing gaps without adding inference machinery.

## Compatibility

Existing v1-v4 bytecode remains executable. The compiler emits v5 for new
balanced-Core forms. Source using direct possibility sampling receives a v5
diagnostic directing the programmer to `distribution(...)`.

## Test coverage

- Constructor, refinement, inspection, sampling, resolution, and failure tests
  cover every cell of the five-form matrix.
- Regression fixtures cover invalid support, invalid weights, impossible
  evidence, opaque inference, Paths restrictions, and legacy joint aliases.
- Verifier and VM tests cover v5 instructions and unchanged v1-v4 loading.
